## Overview

Connect Slack from the Beacon dashboard to send alert notifications to a Slack channel. You can choose either a public channel or a private channel that has the Beacon Slack app added to it.

## Connect Slack

1. Log in to the Beacon dashboard.
2. Go to **Connectors**.
3. Find the Slack integration.
4. Click **Connect**.
5. In Slack, select the workspace where Beacon should send alert notifications.
6. Review the permissions requested by the Beacon app.
7. Click **Allow**.

After Slack redirects back to Beacon, choose the channel where alert notifications should be delivered.

## Choose a notification channel

1. Open the Slack connector in Beacon.
2. Search for the channel by name or channel ID.
3. Select the Slack channel from the dropdown.
4. Click **Save channel**.
5. Click **Send test notification** to confirm Beacon can post to the channel.

## Add private channels

Slack only allows apps to post in private channels after the app has been added to that channel.

1. Open the private Slack channel.
2. Click the channel name to open channel details.
3. Go to **Integrations**.
4. Click **Add apps**.
5. Search for **Beacon**.
6. Add the Beacon app to the channel.
7. Return to the Slack connector in Beacon.
8. Search for the private channel by name or channel ID.
9. Select it, then click **Save channel**.
10. Click **Send test notification** to confirm delivery.

<Frame caption="Add the Beacon app to a private Slack channel before selecting it in the connector.">
  <img src="/images/slack_private_channel.png" alt="Slack private channel integrations screen showing the Beacon app added to the channel." />
</Frame>

If the private channel does not appear immediately, refresh the Beacon connector page and search again. Make sure the Beacon app is a member of the private channel before selecting it.
